Start with five small tasks

  1. Play. Save one screenshot and explain two controls and the goal.
  2. Choose one character, background, object or sound. Explain its job.
  3. Generate it, revise the prompt once and save both attempts.
  4. Log the actual dates, exact tool/model, full prompts, settings, reference links, output links and version filenames. Explain which version you prefer.
  5. Test again. Record one successful test or a problem with steps to repeat it. Request teacher review.

  • Hold F to fire. Aim with I/up, J/left, K/down, L/right; combine for diagonals.
  • Without aim keys you shoot where you face. Move with WASD or arrows.
  • Walk around cover; this mode has no jumping.
  • Turrets flash yellow before firing. Their pink shots hurt; cyan shots are yours.
  • Collect the repair core, clear both turrets and reach the gold exit.
  • R resets health, enemies and items. Escape returns home without a reward.

Read the code with your teacher

Your file is game/js/students/world_052/pack.js. A line starting with // is an explanation, not a command.

  • manifest identifies your game and house. Keep its IDs fixed.
  • home describes your room. Positions here use tiles.
  • dialogue contains your guide’s words. Try changing one quoted sentence with teacher help.
  • level describes your game. Positions here use pixels; objectives and rules connect through IDs.

The teacher maintains engine files. Make only one demonstrated change at a time, keep the previous version, and test before making another change. Do not paste an AI replacement for the whole engine.

Assets, tests and revisions

Save assets beside pack.js in assets/. They do not appear automatically: your teacher helps check dimensions, transparency, filenames and code use. Record every prompt attempt, including rejected versions. Keep Unknown/Not shown for missing historical dates or hidden model versions.

In Revisions, record the revision number, date, changed files, reason and test result. In Bugs and tests, record what you expected, what happened and how to repeat it. F3 shows pack errors; F1 toggles debug information.
